# NUI Flagship Experiential Website — Design Specification

## Status
Approved at concept level by the user. The selected direction is **Living Intelligence** with the restraint, typography discipline, and quiet-space control of a premium institutional/editorial system. This specification records the design before implementation.

## Product surface
A flagship public website for Nolane / Nolane UI Intelligence that demonstrates the product by making its intelligence architecture visible and experiential. The site is not a generic documentation shell and not a component gallery. It is a cinematic, scroll-driven narrative whose central visual object is a living representation of the NUI system.

## User intent preserved
Source intent to preserve throughout implementation and critique:
- “cực kì đẹp” / extremely beautiful
- “hiệu ứng cực kì mạnh” / extremely strong effects
- “càng kéo xuống dưới, nó càng cực kì đẹp” / visual experience should become increasingly impressive through the scroll journey
- “xứng đáng với giá trị 100000 đô” / perceived craft should be comparable to a high-budget bespoke digital experience

These are affective requirements, not permission to add indiscriminate spectacle.

## Experiential invariants
### Desired feelings
- awe
- discovery
- intelligence becoming visible
- magnitude
- precision
- authorship
- sophistication
- controlled power
- memorability

### Forbidden feelings
- generic AI SaaS
- cheap cyberpunk

- component-library collage
- motion sickness
- noisy tech demo
- decorative neural-network cliché
- artificial complexity without meaning
- dark UI whose only claim to premium is low luminance

### Identity projection
The visitor should feel that Nolane is not merely another UI library or static skill collection, but a coherent intelligence system with routing, evidence, critique, research, and synthesis operating as one architecture.

## Visual ambition
Classification: **EXCEPTIONAL + EXPERIENTIAL**.

High-ambition routes are mandatory: experiential intent preservation, aesthetic divergence, visual-reference research, hierarchy, typography, color, rhythm, depth/surfaces, imagery, motion, genericity review, visual-energy control, signature-depth validation, rendered iteration, design-fidelity verification, runtime verification, accessibility/reduced-motion review, and aesthetic-adequacy critique.

## Selected art direction
### Living Intelligence
The site begins with near-silence and one visually minimal seed. As the user scrolls, that seed evolves into a progressively richer intelligence field. The same object persists across the entire experience and changes state rather than being replaced by unrelated section effects.

The progression is:

`seed → relation → network → routed system → evidence field → critic loop → dimensional intelligence architecture → spatial climax → resolution`

The visual language borrows no single upstream product identity. It may synthesize premium editorial restraint, scientific visualization precision, cinematic transition craft, and generative procedural media, but all mechanisms must be re-authored around NUI product truth.

## Signature mechanism: Nolane Intelligence Field
The Intelligence Field is the site’s persistent signature object.

### Causal chain
- Product truth: NUI is a graph of specialized skills, routes, sources, evidence, critics, and synthesis relationships.
- User meaning: intelligence is systemic rather than isolated in one model or one component.
- Interaction/information behavior: the field expands as the narrative introduces new NUI capabilities; connections, routing, evidence gaps, critics, and synthesis become visible through state changes.
- Visual expression: one evolving generative object with topology, depth, luminosity, motion, and spatial scale tied to real semantic categories.
- Emotional consequence: the visitor experiences increasing scope, coherence, and intelligence rather than merely reading feature claims.

### Signature depth requirements
The mechanism must not be reproducible as a generic “particle sphere” by swapping branding. Its structure and transitions must depend on NUI concepts and data. Removing it must materially reduce comprehension, identity, and emotional reward.

### Signature rhythm
- Primary: opening seed, architecture reveal, spatial climax, final resolution.
- Supporting: capability transitions and routing demonstrations.
- Absent/quiet: dense reading moments, accessibility content, text-heavy explanation, and deliberate rest zones.

## Spatial dramaturgy
The page is designed as a sequence of spatial beats rather than stacked sections.

### Beat 1 — Silence (0–8%)
- Nearly empty viewport.
- Deep dark material field, not flat black.
- One small luminous seed.
- Minimal navigation presence.
- Hero statement with highly controlled type.
- Motion is subtle and responsive, not decorative noise.

### Beat 2 — First Awakening (8–20%)
- Seed begins creating relations.
- Typography and field behavior establish visual language.
- The first semantic relation appears so the visitor understands the field is meaningful.

### Beat 3 — Cognitive Architecture (20–35%)
- The field organizes into recognizable territories.
- Regions correspond to NUI faculties such as product reasoning, craft, research, evidence, critics, and verification.
- Interaction remains comprehensible without requiring hover.

### Beat 4 — Scale Break (35–50%)
- Compression gives way to a large open field.
- Camera and layout reveal depth beyond the first apparent layer.
- Chrome recedes to increase spatial magnitude.

### Beat 5 — Intelligence in Motion (50–65%)
- Motion communicates routing, propagation, criticism, and evidence state.
- A route can branch, fail, re-evaluate, and resolve.
- Animation has information semantics; ambient movement remains subordinate.

### Beat 6 — World Opens (65–78%)
- The viewport becomes the experience rather than a page containing a canvas.
- 2D content and 3D/generative field are compositionally integrated.
- Typography, depth, and camera movement form one authored spatial scene.

### Beat 7 — Spatial Climax (78–90%)
- One decisive monumental moment.
- The full Intelligence Field reveals itself as one coherent architecture.
- Visual energy, scale, depth, light, and motion peak together without becoming chaotic.
- No second equivalent climax elsewhere on the site.

### Beat 8 — Resolution (90–100%)
- Energy decays deliberately.
- The large architecture collapses or resolves into a refined, recognizable Nolane form.
- Final proposition and CTA are restrained because the journey has already established value.

## Visual energy topology
Visual complexity may increase through the journey, but energy must have peaks and quiet fields.

Target qualitative energy curve:
`10 → 16 → 27 → 43 → 58 → 72 → 86 → 100 → 45`

Energy channels include scale, luminance excursion, chroma mass, depth, information velocity, motion, image detail, and material contrast. High ambition does not imply neon saturation everywhere.

## Material progression
A staged material system reinforces the sense of awakening:

`matte → translucent → luminous → volumetric → resolved`

This progression must influence surfaces, type contrast, field treatment, depth, and light behavior. It is not simply a stack of blur filters.

## Typography
Typography must carry premium authorship even with all graphics disabled.

Requirements:
- one expressive display strategy for monumental statements
- one highly legible text strategy for explanatory copy
- large-scale typography used sparingly for spatial events, not every heading
- variable type features may be used where they materially contribute to transitions or scale
- no microtext used as artificial sophistication
- responsive typography preserves hierarchy and dramaturgical role rather than only shrinking desktop sizes

## Color
The primary field is dark and controlled, but the palette must not collapse into gray-on-black minimalism.

Color behavior:
- quiet structural neutrals dominate early
- chroma appears as meaningful focal energy rather than blanket gradient
- semantic NUI states may own limited, consistent color roles
- luminous events become stronger as system complexity becomes visible
- final resolution lowers energy without losing richness
- all critical meaning remains available without color alone

## Motion constitution
Every material animation must map to one or more approved purposes:
- causality
- continuity
- hierarchy
- spatial origin
- progress
- feedback
- expressive identity

Ambient motion has a strict budget.

Requirements:
- scroll remains interruptible
- user can reverse direction without corrupted state
- no mandatory decorative intro before content becomes usable
- animations must retarget/cancel cleanly under rapid input
- no large uncontrolled vestibular motion in reduced-motion mode
- semantic state changes remain understandable when motion is reduced

## Technology architecture
### Primary language
**TypeScript**.

Rationale: strongest combination of web platform reach, typed component architecture, React ecosystem maturity, Three.js tooling, animation orchestration, testing, and maintainability for a site whose visual system is interactive rather than pre-rendered.

### Framework and runtime
- Next.js with React and TypeScript
- App Router
- React Three Fiber + Three.js for the Intelligence Field
- Drei only where it reduces boilerplate without dictating aesthetics
- GSAP + ScrollTrigger for high-control scroll choreography and timeline coordination
- Motion for local UI transitions/microinteractions when it is simpler than GSAP
- custom GLSL shaders for signature materials and field behavior where CSS/stock materials cannot express the thesis
- modern CSS with design tokens and container-responsive behavior

### Ownership rules
- Three/R3F owns the persistent spatial field.
- GSAP owns long-form scroll choreography and camera/scene sequencing.
- Motion owns isolated component transitions where no shared timeline is required.
- CSS owns layout, typography, surfaces, states, and non-spatial visual behavior.
- No library owns art direction.

## Repository architecture
The existing root remains the NUI knowledge/skill repository. The site is isolated in:

`website/`

Planned major boundaries:
- `website/app/` — Next.js routes and page shell
- `website/components/experience/` — scroll chapters and narrative composition
- `website/components/field/` — R3F scene and Intelligence Field state
- `website/components/ui/` — semantic UI controls, navigation, CTA, text surfaces
- `website/lib/motion/` — timeline contracts and scroll state
- `website/lib/field/` — topology generation, field semantic model, adaptive quality logic
- `website/lib/nui/` — curated build-time NUI content/data transformed for visualization
- `website/styles/` — tokens, global typography, materials, responsive contracts
- `website/public/` — authored static media only
- `website/tests/` — unit/component tests
- `website/e2e/` — viewport, runtime, motion, and accessibility smoke tests

The website must consume NUI source data through a deliberately normalized representation rather than scraping arbitrary prose at runtime.

## Adaptive rendering
The experience must scale down gracefully.

### Capability tiers
- High: full field density, advanced shader treatment, richer postprocessing, full depth choreography.
- Medium: reduced particles/edges, simpler shader paths, limited postprocessing.
- Low: reduced or static 3D field with high-quality 2D/canvas fallback.
- Reduced motion: semantic state progression without large parallax/camera travel or continuous ambient movement.

Tier selection must be based on capability/performance evidence, not device-name assumptions alone.

## Responsive dramaturgy
Mobile is not desktop squeezed smaller.

Desktop may use panoramic space, side-by-side content/field relationships, and longer camera travel. Mobile converts panoramic beats into staged sequences, tighter crops, sheets, and controlled progress while preserving the same narrative role: silence, awakening, scale break, causality, climax, resolution.

## Accessibility
- semantic DOM content exists independent of WebGL
- keyboard navigation remains complete
- screen readers receive meaningful textual explanation rather than canvas-only content
- reduced-motion semantic equivalence is mandatory
- contrast is validated for all critical text and controls
- canvas interactions must never be the only route to essential information
- pointer-only affordances are prohibited for required content
- visual effects must avoid unsafe flashing patterns

## Performance targets
Budgets are implementation gates rather than aspirations.

Initial targets:
- no long-running main-thread animation loop work unrelated to visible state
- dynamic import for heavy 3D client code
- no layout-shifting webfont behavior visible in the hero
- no persistent off-screen simulation at full quality
- postprocessing disabled or degraded when frame stability falls below the selected tier threshold
- field complexity adapts to viewport and measured frame budget
- route is usable before the full experience layer hydrates

Performance will be tested in throttled conditions; visual ambition cannot waive interaction responsiveness.

## Content architecture
The narrative should explain NUI through a limited number of meaningful ideas rather than list 158 skills.

Core content themes:
1. Intelligence as a system
2. Specialized faculties
3. Routing instead of monolithic prompting
4. Evidence and source authority
5. Critics and falsification
6. Cross-source synthesis
7. Verification and release discipline
8. NUI as an intelligence layer over the UI ecosystem

Detailed skill counts and exhaustive catalogues belong in secondary surfaces or documentation, not the cinematic narrative.

## Anti-generic constraints
Explicitly reject:
- repeated bento grids as page structure
- gradient blobs with no semantic role
- random neural particles
- generic rotating 3D sphere
- feature-card repetition as primary storytelling
- glass everywhere
- meaningless terminal/system labels
- fake coordinates/rulers
- every section using the same centered heading formula
- every element using scroll fade-up
- cursor-following ornament without interaction value
- copying Aceternity/React Bits demos as the final language

A blind product-substitution test must fail: swapping the Nolane name for an unrelated AI startup should make the experience semantically incoherent.

## Release criteria
The site cannot be called complete while any of these remain unresolved:
- signature mechanism is visually impressive but semantically generic
- visual direction loses against a materially different candidate without evidence advantage
- reduced-motion removes essential meaning
- mobile loses the spatial/narrative function of major beats
- frame instability materially harms interaction
- critical information exists only inside WebGL
- final result resembles generic AI SaaS under blind substitution
- aesthetic adequacy critic returns INADEQUATE or RE_DIVERGE

## Scope boundary for first implementation
The first milestone builds one complete flagship landing experience with the eight beats above, one persistent Intelligence Field, full responsive/reduced-motion behavior, and release evidence. Secondary documentation pages, blog, authentication, CMS, and marketing-growth infrastructure are intentionally outside the first milestone unless required to support the flagship route.

## Success definition
A successful result is not merely “a very animated site.” It must remain recognizably Nolane with branding partially removed; become richer and more consequential through scroll; create one memorable spatial climax; remain usable and intelligible without WebGL or large motion; and demonstrate enough authored craft that replacing it with a conventional premium SaaS template would cause a clear loss of product meaning, identity, and emotional impact.
