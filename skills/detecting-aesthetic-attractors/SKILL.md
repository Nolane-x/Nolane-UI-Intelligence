# Detecting Aesthetic Attractors

## Parent Contract
**Required parent:** `preventing-generic-ui`.

This child strengthens the parent and may not waive parent obligations.

## Decision Boundary
Own global accumulation analysis across mechanisms. Do not ban named styles categorically. Judge repeated mechanisms using semantic necessity, subject_specificity, frequency, information gain, emotional contribution, and removal_cost.

## Product Truth
Genericity is often emergent. Tiny mono metadata, cyan accents, hairline grids, coordinate labels, topology circles, borders, terminal vocabulary, or muted palettes can each be legitimate once; dozens of low-specificity repetitions become an aesthetic attractor. Local justification cannot dismiss systemic frequency.

## Decision Model
Inventory mechanisms across the rendered product. Escalate when frequency is high while subject_specificity, semantic necessity, and removal_cost are low. Also inspect `boundary_density`, `edge_density`, `surface_entropy`, `boundary_repetition`, `material_variety`, and `quiet_region_ratio` to catch border/pane soup. Run a counterfactual specificity test: mask brand nouns/logo and ask whether the same visual system could serve an unrelated SOC, quant desk, spacecraft console, or generic AI dashboard. Add an anti-timidity check at flagship+: absence of any memorable mechanism is itself a finding unless deliberate quietness is demonstrably distinctive.

## Evidence
Return mechanism ledger, counts, global metrics, probable-trope findings, counterfactual result, and remediation direction. Evidence must aggregate across screens rather than cite isolated components.

## Output Contract: `aesthetic-attractor-audit`
Return the canonical `aesthetic-attractor-audit` artifact with explicit status, evidence references, unresolved unknowns, and downstream routes. Missing material evidence must remain UNKNOWN/BLOCKED rather than being inferred from confidence.

## Failure Traps
Hard-banning cyberpunk; counting one border as a violation; allowing 100 tiny labels because each has a local reason; replacing card soup with pane soup; anti-generic rules that force every design to be loud; style-name policing without subject-specificity evidence.
