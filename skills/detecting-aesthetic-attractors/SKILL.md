# Detecting Aesthetic Attractors

## Parent Contract
**Required parent:** `preventing-generic-ui`.

This child strengthens the parent and may not waive parent obligations.

## Decision Boundary
Own global accumulation analysis across mechanisms. Do not ban named styles categorically. Judge repeated mechanisms using semantic necessity, subject_specificity, frequency, information gain, emotional contribution, and removal_cost.

## Product Truth
Genericity is often emergent. Tiny mono metadata, cyan accents, hairline grids, coordinate labels, topology circles, borders, terminal vocabulary, or muted palettes can each be legitimate once; dozens of low-specificity repetitions become an aesthetic attractor. Local justification cannot dismiss systemic frequency.

## Decision Model
Inventory mechanisms across the rendered product. Escalate when frequency is high while subject_specificity, semantic necessity, and removal_cost are low. Also inspect `boundary_density`, `edge_density`, `surface_entropy`, `boundary_repetition`, `material_variety`, and `quiet_region_ratio` to catch border/pane soup. Run a counterfactual specificity test: mask brand nouns/logo and ask whether the same visual system could serve an unrelated SOC, quant desk, spacecraft console, or generic AI dashboard. Add an anti-timidity check at flagship+: absence of any memorable mechanism is itself a finding unless deliberate quietness is demonstrably distinctive.

## Evidence
Return mechanism ledger, counts, global metrics, probable-trope findings, counterfactual result, and remediation direction. Evidence must aggregate across screens rather than cite isolated components.

## Output Contract: `aesthetic-attractor-audit`
Return the canonical `aesthetic-attractor-audit` artifact with explicit status, evidence references, unresolved unknowns, and downstream routes. Missing material evidence must remain UNKNOWN/BLOCKED rather than being inferred from confidence.

## Failure Traps
Hard-banning cyberpunk; counting one border as a violation; allowing 100 tiny labels because each has a local reason; replacing card soup with pane soup; anti-generic rules that force every design to be loud; style-name policing without subject-specificity evidence.

## V6 Attractor Field Analysis
Audit the UI as a population of mechanisms, not isolated components. Build a co-occurrence table for rounded containers, hairline borders, muted mono microtext, glow, gradients, glass, floating cards, pills, dotted grids, tiny labels, oversized hero type, icon buttons, hover lift, noise, charts, and any project-specific motif. **Mechanism co-occurrence** matters because several individually defensible choices can combine into a recognizable AI/default style.

Measure **attractor concentration**: how much of the interface's identity is explained by the top repeated mechanisms? High concentration is not automatically wrong—strong art direction often repeats—but each repeated mechanism needs semantic necessity, subject specificity, emotional contribution, information gain, and a meaningful removal cost.

Estimate **aesthetic entropy** qualitatively across geometry, material, typography, density, media, interaction and spatial rhythm. Very low entropy can signal coherent restraint or deadening sameness; very high entropy can signal expressive richness or incoherent collage. Interpret entropy against the experiential intent and task structure, never as a universal numerical optimum.

Use the **removal-cost test**: remove a dominant mechanism across representative surfaces. If no task, meaning, emotional identity, hierarchy or signature is lost, it is likely stylistic residue. If removal improves clarity without reducing character, the mechanism was accumulating debt.

Run an **anti-timidity countercheck** after every anti-generic pass. Agents often respond to “avoid gradients/cards/glass/motion” by making everything beige, flat, gray and cautious. Ask whether the product still has a memorable authored mechanism and sufficient visual energy for its ambition. Anti-slop is not permission for anti-beauty.

### Falsification
Blind the product name and substitute domain copy. If the interface instantly reads as generic AI dashboard/crypto terminal/agency landing page regardless of subject, the attractor is stronger than product specificity. Conversely, if a repeated mechanism is strongly tied to domain semantics and removal causes measurable loss, retain it even if fashionable.

### Recovery
For excessive attractor concentration, do not randomly diversify components. Identify the lowest-necessity high-frequency mechanism and redesign the system relation it is standing in for. For low-energy timidity, reopen signature, media, color energy or spatial dramaturgy instead of reintroducing arbitrary effects.
