# Directing Visual Ambition

## Parent Contract
**Required parent:** `routing-ui-work`.

This child strengthens the parent and may not waive parent obligations.

## Decision Boundary
Own classification of visual ambition and the hard-route policy it activates. Do not choose the art direction itself. The levels are `utilitarian`, `polished`, `distinctive`, `flagship`, `exceptional`, and `experiential`.

## Product Truth
“Make it work” and “make it unforgettable” are not the same workload. Context-saving heuristics are useful at low ambition but become a failure when they skip references, divergence, media, motion, typography, or independent adequacy review for flagship work.

## Decision Model
Classify from explicit language, product importance, novelty freedom, brand constraints, and memorability stakes. For flagship/exceptional/experiential, issue a hard route through aesthetic exploration, reference research, hierarchy, typography, color, spacing/rhythm, surfaces, imagery, motion, anti-generic review, attractor detection, legibility, visual energy, signature depth, execution critic, adequacy critic, rendered iteration, and basin escape. Add visualization, aspirational identity, spatial dramaturgy, and perceptual diversity owners when their triggers exist. A hard route is not optional for context economy.

## Evidence
Record the selected level, triggering phrases/signals, visual freedom, mandatory route set, any inapplicable route with a semantic reason, and escalation/de-escalation conditions. `exceptional` without the hard route is invalid.

## Output Contract: `visual-ambition-contract`
Return the canonical `visual-ambition-contract` artifact with explicit status, evidence references, unresolved unknowns, and downstream routes. Missing material evidence must remain UNKNOWN/BLOCKED rather than being inferred from confidence.

## Failure Traps
Defaulting every serious tool to polished restraint; treating “premium” as enough evidence for exceptional; loading every craft faculty for utilitarian tasks; allowing a router to skip a hard route because the prompt is long; confusing novelty with ambition.
