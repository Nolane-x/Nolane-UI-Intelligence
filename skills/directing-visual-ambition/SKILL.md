# Directing Visual Ambition

## Parent Contract
**Required parent:** `routing-ui-work`.

This child strengthens the parent and may not waive parent obligations.

## Decision Boundary
Own classification of visual ambition and the hard-route policy it activates. Do not choose the art direction itself. The levels are `utilitarian`, `polished`, `distinctive`, `flagship`, `exceptional`, and `experiential`.

## Product Truth
“Make it work” and “make it unforgettable” are not the same workload. Context-saving heuristics are useful at low ambition but become a failure when they skip references, divergence, media, motion, typography, or independent adequacy review for flagship work.

## Decision Model
Classify from explicit language, product importance, novelty freedom, brand constraints, and memorability stakes. For flagship/exceptional/experiential, issue a hard route through aesthetic exploration, reference research, hierarchy, typography, color, spacing/rhythm, surfaces, imagery, motion, anti-generic review, attractor detection, legibility, visual energy, signature depth, execution critic, adequacy critic, rendered iteration, and basin escape. Add visualization, aspirational identity, spatial dramaturgy, and perceptual diversity owners when their triggers exist. A hard route is not optional for context economy.

## Evidence
Record the selected level, triggering phrases/signals, visual freedom, mandatory route set, any inapplicable route with a semantic reason, and escalation/de-escalation conditions. `exceptional` without the hard route is invalid.

## Output Contract: `visual-ambition-contract`
Return the canonical `visual-ambition-contract` artifact with explicit status, evidence references, unresolved unknowns, and downstream routes. Missing material evidence must remain UNKNOWN/BLOCKED rather than being inferred from confidence.

## Failure Traps
Defaulting every serious tool to polished restraint; treating “premium” as enough evidence for exceptional; loading every craft faculty for utilitarian tasks; allowing a router to skip a hard route because the prompt is long; confusing novelty with ambition.

## V6 Ambition Calibration Engine
Visual ambition is a resource allocation decision, not a compliment. Create an **ambition budget** across art direction, custom composition, typography, media, motion, interactive signature, data expression, responsive behavior, and finishing. A flagship product may spend heavily on only three of these if those three carry identity; “exceptional” does not mean maximum spectacle in every dimension.

For each level define a **quality floor** that cannot be traded away: utilitarian still requires legibility and coherence; polished requires deliberate hierarchy and system consistency; distinctive requires a product-specific visual thesis; flagship requires memorable authored mechanisms and reference competition; exceptional requires evidence that the result meaningfully exceeds competent category baselines; experiential additionally requires the emotional/identity journey to survive interaction over time.

Use an **evidence escalation ladder**. As ambition rises, increase the burden of proof: rendered alternatives, diverse reference frontier, custom mechanism depth, computed typography/color evidence, multi-viewport craft, independent adequacy critique, cross-surface diversity, and basin-escape testing. Do not merely add more prose or more UI libraries.

Diagnose **ambition mismatch** in both directions. Under-shoot occurs when a high-ambition brief receives safe framework defaults, one composition, generic type, stock icons and cosmetic motion. Over-shoot occurs when routine transactional work receives cinematic effects that slow tasks, inflate maintenance cost, or violate accessibility/performance. Ambition must match user value, frequency, context, risk, brand maturity and available evidence.

A **de-escalation rule** is legitimate only when a concrete constraint makes the higher ambition harmful or unverifiable: severe performance budget, safety-critical attention limits, fixed institutional design language, release time with no evidence capacity, or explicit user preference. Record the lost experiential value and preserve a future path rather than pretending the lower level is equivalent.

### Falsification
Compare a candidate produced under the selected ambition level against one level lower with labels hidden. If critics cannot identify a material difference in product-specific craft, the ambition contract is not operational. Conversely, if a higher level adds spectacle but no stronger intent fit, memorability, usability or material richness, the allocation is waste.

### Recovery
When the evidence ladder cannot be satisfied, do not self-certify. Either reduce the claim through the de-escalation rule or reopen references, divergence and implementation capacity. Repeated **ambition mismatch** after local polish routes to re-divergence rather than another pass of shadows, gradients or microanimation.
